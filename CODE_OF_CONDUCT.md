# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the LFPAnalysis project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the community and users

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional scientific collaboration.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Contact

To report a concern, open a private security advisory or contact the project maintainer through GitHub.
